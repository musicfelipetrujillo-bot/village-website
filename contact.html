SMS opt-in and opt-out

The Village sends SMS only to phone numbers users provide and only after the user opts in to a specific category in the app under Me → Notification Preferences. SMS categories include appointment reminders, support-room digests, milk-match notifications, AI replies, articles, events, and promotions. Promotions is off by default.

To opt out of all messaging from us, reply STOP to any SMS we send. To get help, reply HELP. Carrier message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for SMS.

Sample messages users may receive after opting in:

  • "Hi {name}, this is The Village. Your appointment with Dr. {provider} is tomorrow at {time}. Reply STOP to opt out."
  • "Good morning — your weekly recap from your support room is ready. Open the app to read it. Reply STOP to opt out."
  • "You have a new milk-match request from a verified Village family. Open the app to review. Reply STOP to opt out."

For full SMS data practices, see the SMS section of our Privacy Policy.
