A village for every mom.

The Village is a mobile app for expecting and postpartum families — a clinician-aware companion for the first year of motherhood. Find vetted specialists, connect with peer breast-milk donors, join private support rooms, browse safety-checked baby gear, and track your baby's milestones week by week.

Not medical advice. The Village provides information, peer connection, and tools. It does not replace professional medical care. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911. If you are in mental-health crisis, dial 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line). Postpartum Support International: 1-800-944-4773.

What's inside

Find specialists

OB/GYN, doulas, midwives, lactation consultants, pediatricians, sleep coaches, pelvic-floor PTs, perinatal dietitians, PPD therapists. Filter by your insurance and search radius.

Milk Connect

A peer-to-peer breast-milk donor marketplace. Verified profiles, voluntary trust badges, informed-consent gates. Not a regulated milk bank — see Terms.

Support rooms

Stage-matched private chat rooms with optional anonymous mode. AI-assisted moderation flags crisis content and routes to trained moderators.

Gear & home

A used-baby-gear marketplace with built-in CPSC recall checks and barcode lookup. Weekly milestones, daily check-ins, and curated brand perks.

SMS communications

The Village sends optional SMS notifications. You opt in per category in the app under Me → Notification Preferences. You can opt out of any category at any time, or reply STOP to any SMS to opt out of all messaging from us. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates from your carrier may apply.

SMS categories include: appointment reminders for clinical bookings, postpartum daily check-in reminders (opt-in), peer milk-match notifications, and crisis-resource referrals when our AI detects safety concerns in support-room messages. Promotional SMS is opt-in only and disabled by default.

For our full SMS data practices, see the SMS section of our Privacy Policy.

Hospital partnerships

The Village is being offered to hospitals to bundle into mom-and-baby discharge packets. Postpartum 0–6 weeks is our primary care window, and our content is written to clinician-handoff standards. Reach out if you represent a hospital, OB practice, or birth center.

Built with care

The Village is independently operated and free for families. We do not sell your personal information. We are not a HIPAA-covered entity, but we apply HIPAA-aligned controls to all health-adjacent data: row-level security on every database table, encryption at rest and in transit, and minimum-necessary access. See the Privacy Policy for specifics.
